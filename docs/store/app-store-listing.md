## Description

Headwind is a free, modern electronic flight bag for general-aviation
pilots. No subscription, no accounts, no ads — just great tools on top of
official FAA and NOAA data.

CHARTS & MOVING MAP
• Clean aeronautical map with vector Class B/C/D airspace
• FAA VFR Sectional, Terminal, IFR Low and High chart layers
• Offline chart downloads for flying without signal
• Live TFRs drawn on the map
• Ownship position with ground speed, track, and altitude

WEATHER
• Live METARs and TAFs with VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR color coding
• Winds and temperatures aloft
• Density altitude and a per-runway head/crosswind advisor
• On-device AI briefings in plain English (Apple Intelligence), always
  grounded in the actual observations

PLANNING
• Every US airport (16,000+) and navaid — route with airports and VORs
• Wind-corrected headings, ground speeds, time and fuel per leg
• Weight & balance with an interactive CG envelope
• Approach plates and airport diagrams for 3,000+ airports, offline-ready

PILOT'S KIT
• Digital logbook with totals
• Phase-of-flight checklists including emergencies
• Aeronautical data that updates itself every FAA cycle

Headwind is open source (MIT) and built for iOS 26 with Liquid Glass design.

Headwind is not certified for navigation and is not a substitute for
official charts, weather briefings, or NOTAMs. The pilot in command is
solely responsible for the safe conduct of every flight.
