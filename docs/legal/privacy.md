# Headwind Privacy Policy

_Effective: June 2026_

Headwind is a free, open-source flight-planning app for pilots. Our privacy
posture is simple: **we don't want your data, so we don't take it.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Headwind has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no
tracking, and no server of our own. We cannot see who you are, where you fly,
or how you use the app.

## Location

Headwind asks for location permission solely to show your position on the
moving map, display ground speed/track/altitude, and list nearby airports.
Your location is processed **entirely on your device**. It is never
transmitted to us or to anyone else, never stored beyond the app's own
on-device caches, and never linked to your identity.

## Data stored on your device

Flight plans, logbook entries, aircraft profiles, downloaded charts and
plates, and cached weather live only on your device (and, where you enable
it, your personal iCloud). Deleting the app deletes them.

## Third-party services the app talks to

To show you aviation data, Headwind fetches from public government services:

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| aviationweather.gov (NOAA) | METARs, TAFs, winds aloft |
| tfr.faa.gov (FAA) | Temporary Flight Restrictions |
| FAA chart tile services (ArcGIS) | Sectional/terminal/IFR chart imagery |
| aeronav.faa.gov (FAA) | Approach plate PDFs |
| raw.githubusercontent.com | Updated procedure indexes each data cycle |

These requests necessarily include your IP address and the stations/areas you
request, as any web request does. We add no identifiers to them. Each service
is operated by its respective agency under its own policies.

## Apple Intelligence

AI briefing summaries are generated by Apple's on-device foundation model.
Your weather data does not leave your device for this feature.

## Children

Headwind is a pilot's tool and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published here and noted in release
notes.
