# Headwind Support

Headwind is free and open source. Support happens in the open, on GitHub.

## Get help / report a bug

Open an issue: <https://github.com/dbhagwan/headwind/issues>

Please include your iOS version, device, what you expected, and what
happened. Screenshots help enormously.

## Frequently asked

**Is Headwind certified for navigation?**
No. Headwind is a planning and situational-awareness aid. It is not a
substitute for official charts, a regulatory weather briefing, or NOTAMs.
The pilot in command is solely responsible for every flight.

**How current is the data?**
Airports, navaids, and approach plates follow the FAA's 28-day cycles and are
regenerated automatically each cycle. Settings shows the active cycle and its
validity dates; the app warns you when data is stale and updates the
procedure index automatically.

**Why does Headwind ask for my location?**
Only to show your position on the map and find nearby airports. Location is
processed on-device and never transmitted — see the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Does it work offline?**
Yes, that's a design goal: downloaded charts, viewed plates, the full airport
database, and your last-fetched weather (clearly age-labeled) all work
without connectivity.

**How is this free?**
Every data source Headwind uses — FAA charts, plates, airports, NOAA weather —
is published free by the US government. There's no server to pay for and no
subscription to sell. The code is MIT-licensed.
